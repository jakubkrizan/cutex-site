Privacy Policy

Cutex 1.1 · last updated 2026-11-26

The short version. Cutex runs on your own computer. There is no Cutex account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is sent to the people who made Cutex.

Your recordings, chats, clips and settings stay in the data folder on this PC. Data only leaves the PC when you use a feature that needs another service (Twitch, YouTube, an AI service...) - listed below, with what is sent.

1What is stored on your computer

Everything is kept in your own Windows user folder, %LOCALAPPDATA%\Cutex - the data folder (and logs for the log files). It stays when you update or reinstall Cutex:

  • Recordings of the streams you clip, and the chat messages recorded with them (usernames and message text). Full recordings are deleted automatically after recording.keep_days days (3 by default).
  • Clips and the untouched 16:9 part of each clip, with their transcripts (what was said, made on this PC), titles, hooks, topics and summaries, scores and thumbnails.
  • Your settings, the list of streamers you added, your music and custom videos, and the text of the logs.
  • If you connect YouTube: the Google client secret you pasted and a login token (youtube_client_secret.json, youtube_token.json). Anyone who can read the data folder can use that token, so keep the folder private.

3Other people's data

Clipping streams means handling other people's voices, faces and chat messages. Cutex keeps them only on your PC (see 1), but what you do with the clips is up to you:

  • Only clip streamers who allow it, and keep the credit on the clip.
  • Don't publish chat usernames or personal details. Use the Hide (blur) tool in the editor for promo codes, links or anything private on screen.
  • Music has its own copyright: only use songs you have the right to use.

4How long data is kept, and how to delete it

  • Full recordings are deleted after recording.keep_days days. Clips, chat logs, music and settings stay until you delete them.
  • Delete a clip, a song or a custom video with its trash button in the app.
  • To remove everything: close Cutex and delete the data and logs folders. If you connected YouTube, also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

5Security

  • The dashboard is only reachable from this computer (127.0.0.1). The restart and stop commands only answer requests from this PC.
  • The dashboard has no password. If you make it reachable from other devices (for example with dashboard.host: 0.0.0.0 in Docker), everyone on that network can see and delete your clips - only do that on a network you trust.
  • API keys are read from environment variables, not stored by Cutex.

6Children

Cutex is a tool for creators and is not directed at children.

7Your rights

Because your data stays on your own computer, you already have full control: you can look at it in the data folder, correct it, export it (the Clips page can download clips) and delete it at any time (see 5 above). If you live in the EU/UK (GDPR) or California (CCPA) you also have the right to ask any company that holds data about you to show it, correct it, delete it, limit its use or move it, and to complain to your data-protection authority. Cutex's operator does not receive or keep your data, so there is nothing to request from them - but the services Cutex connects to (Twitch, Google, ...) do, and you can use those rights with them.

People who appear in clips (streamers, chat users) can ask you to remove a clip; for copyright, see Copyright & DMCA.

8Changes and contact

If Cutex changes what it stores or sends, this page changes with it - the date at the top tells you when. Cutex is run by whoever installed it, so this page describes the software; if you let other people use your copy, add your own contact details here (edit sc/privacy_page.py).
