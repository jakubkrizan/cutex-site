FAQ

Where does my data go?

Recordings, clips and chat stay on your own computer in a local data folder. Cutex only talks to a service when a feature needs it (Twitch, YouTube, the licence check) - see the Privacy Policy.

What do I need to run it?

Windows, a GPU helps for faster transcription but isn't required, and ffmpeg (the installer fetches it for you).

Am I allowed to clip a streamer?

Only clip streams and use music/video you have permission to use - see Copyright & DMCA and Acceptable Use.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes - open the License page in the app or use the customer portal link in your purchase e-mail.
